Why this matters
- The shell invoice exists from second zero, so:
  - `invoice.status` is visible immediately
  - contractor simulator / admin simulator can show a stable overall row during staging
  - early `ocr_read` and `triage_classifier` step history can be shown before invoice-model OCR begins

Expected coarse invoice status mapping during bundle intake
- `upload_in_progress`
  - shell invoice created; files are being staged
- `ocr_in_progress`
  - DI `read` across staged files
  - per-file triage classifier
  - exactly-one-invoice enforcement
  - invoice-model OCR loopback for the resolved invoice
- `genai_in_progress`
  - code-fact build
  - common GenAI
  - per-upgrade-type GenAI
- terminal:
  - `genai_complete`
  - `ocr_failed`
  - `genai_failed`

Simulator / tracker read-side expectation
- The simulator step-history view is now supposed to show a merged timeline:
  - staged-file steps from `claims.ingest_step_runs.ingest_document_id`
  - later invoice steps from `claims.ingest_step_runs.invoice_version_id`
- If the first visible step is `classifier` and the overall invoice row is blank for ~30 seconds, the read side is wrong-shaped again.
- The correct early staging experience is:
  - invoice row exists immediately
  - invoice status is non-blank immediately
  - earliest visible step should usually be `ocr_read`, not `classifier`
