Official Rebate calculation:

step 1: For each line in the invoice do these steps: 
1) full_unit_subtotal = hardware price per Unit + labour price per unit
Note: labour price per unit is often calculated as labour-total / quantity-of-units
2) full_unit_after_tax_Subtotal = full_unit_subtotal * 1.05
3) rebate_percentage set as either 95 or 60 depending on ESP1 or ESP2 
4) rebate_subtotal = full_unit_after_tax_Subtotal * rebate_percentage
5) rebate_per_line = rebate_subtotal * quantity of units
6) rebate_per_line is capped at 950 per window or door 

Step 2: 
1) invoice_total_rebate_temp = Now sum all the rebate_per_line for the invoice
2) capped_invoice_total_rebate = the invoice total rebate at 9500 (this is a per home cap)

Official Customer portion calculation:

afterrebate_invoicecost = total_invoice_cost - capped_invoice_total_rebate
customer_portion = afterrebate_invoicecost – customer_deposit
if positive then the homeowner / customer still owes the contractor
If negative then the contractor owes the customer

Location tasks:
1 [field_key: contractor_gst_number] Locate contractor GST number within the invoice.
2 [field_key: eligibility_code] Locate the eligibility code (starts with ESP1/ESP2/ESP3). Return a single value if present.
3 [field_key: nrcan_number] Locate NRCan ENERGY STAR fenestration registration number (pattern like NR####-######-ES#) if present and singular.
4 [field_key: cpd_number] Locate CPD (NFRC Certified Products Directory) identifier. looks like something-something-number. Locate if present and singular
5 [field_key: product_brand_model] Locate brand and model of the windows/doors if present. Return a record of {brand, model, description} (empty allowed).
6 [field_key: metric_u_factor] Locate the metric U-factor (numeric, metric) on the invoice if present.
7 [field_key: customer_deposit] Locate the customer deposit (single amount) if present
8 [field_key: labour_cost_total] Locate the total invoice labour cost (single amount) if present or derivable.
9 [field_key: labour_cost_breakdown] Locate labour breakdown per line item/per unit (array). Each entry includes {description, qty, labour_total, labour_per_unit} (empty array allowed).

Rulecheck tasks:

1: Official guidance is that the vendor name on the invoice paper must match the expected name in our ESP database. So, check that di_ocr_vendor_name and di_ocr_vendor_address fuzzy match contractors.business_name and contractors.address.

2: Official guidance is that the customer name on the invoice paper must match the expected name in our ESP database. So, check that di_ocr_customer_name and di_ocr_billing_address fuzzy match users.participant_name and users.participant_address

3: official guidance is that "The rebate application and supporting documentation must be submitted by the Registered Contractor within six (6) months of the invoice date." So, check that sessions.submitted_at <= di_ocr_invoice_date + 6 months

4: official guidance is that "Eligibility codes for applications received on or after June 18, 2024, are valid for upgrades completed within 6 months of the participants approval date." So, check that di_ocr_invoice_date <= users_eligibilitycodes.approved_at + 6 months

5: A sufficiently detailed description of the work performed must be included in the invoice I order for the admins to validate a rebate. Locate the description in the DI JSON (using either the result.content or the first class fields from DI as you see fit) and check it is sufficiently detailed.

6: Check that the rebate calculation on the invoice matches the official-rebate-calculation.

7: Check that the customer-portion-calculation on the invoice matches the official-customer-portion-calculation
