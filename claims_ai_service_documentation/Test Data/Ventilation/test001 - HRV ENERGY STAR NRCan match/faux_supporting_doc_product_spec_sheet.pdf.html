Product Specification Sheet

PRODUCT SPECIFICATION SHEET

Manufacturer / Brand: Airflow
Product type: Heat Recovery Ventilator (HRV)
Model number: AIR205-R
Model type: H

ENERGY STAR Certification

The Airflow AIR205-R heat recovery ventilator is ENERGY STAR certified for Canada.
The model is listed on Natural Resources Canada's searchable product list for ENERGY STAR heat/energy recovery ventilators.

Performance Data

Sensible heat-recovery efficiency (SRE) at 0 C: 76.0%
Sensible heat-recovery efficiency (SRE) at -25 C: 70.0%
Associated net supply airflow at 0 C: 64 CFM / 30 L/s
Associated net supply airflow at -25 C: 102 CFM / 48 L/s
Associated power consumption at 0 C: 61 W
Associated power consumption at -25 C: 104 W
Maximum rated airflow at 0 C: 64 CFM / 30 L/s

Installation

Product is intended for continuous balanced residential ventilation.
Install in accordance with local code and the BC Housing Heat Recovery Ventilation Guide for Houses.
