Existing issue and association
The pre-existing issue was localized mould and moisture staining on attic roof sheathing and adjacent insulation around the planned air-handler platform and duct-access route. Remediation was required before, and completed in direct association with, the eligible central ducted heat-pump installation on the same invoice.

Work Completed
  • Isolated the affected attic work zone and protected the occupied space below.
  • HEPA vacuumed loose debris and surface growth from roof sheathing and framing.
  • Applied approved fungicidal cleaner to localized affected surfaces and removed visibly affected loose insulation debris.
  • Allowed treated surfaces to dry and completed a final visual inspection before HVAC installation.
  • Released the air-handler platform and duct route for safe heat-pump installation.
Completion conclusion
The photographed existing mould issue was remediated before heat-pump installation. The before and after images depict the same attic work area, roof framing, sheathing and baffle layout.
