Private Experience: We have already built real hands-on experience in private AI, so this is no longer new territory for us. Our team has stood up and worked with the core components needed to run private AI responsibly, including the model-serving environment, guardrails, and the surrounding infrastructure. That gives us a strong practical foundation and growing in-house expertise that we can bring directly to customer environments.

Transferable Skills: We are confident in our ability to support this space because the core skills are transferable across AI platforms and hosting models. As the technology evolves, we are comfortable evolving with it, and we are not concerned about differences between one platform and another. Our focus is on providing stable, secure, enterprise-grade support for the underlying environment that makes these services reliable.

Self Service: We fully support a self-service model for the client's AI use at the application and business level. Our role is not to get in the way of innovation, but to provide the lower-level infrastructure support that keeps the platform secure, available, and supportable, just as we do for storage, virtualization, container platforms, networking, and security services today. That creates a clear operating model: the client can move quickly with AI, while we provide the foundational support behind the scenes.

Support Model: Our intent is not to introduce a per-token, cloud-style consumption billing model for this service. We are looking at supporting the AI software at the infrastructure level, in much the same way we support virtual machines and other core platforms today. Rather than tying costs to every unit of usage, we would expect to work with the client on a billing approach that fits their operating model, gives them predictability, and reflects the service as an enterprise infrastructure capability rather than a metered cloud utility.

Client Partnership: We see ourselves as a long-term partner to the client, and our investment in private AI research and development is driven by that mindset. We are building these skills proactively so we can be the trusted infrastructure support team they can rely on as AI becomes a more important part of their environment. We recognize that this kind of role is never guaranteed, and that it has to be earned through initiative, capability, and relevance. That is exactly why we are doing the work now: to stay ahead of the change, deepen our expertise, and be ready to support the client in this new era of AI with credibility and confidence.

Service Evolution: This service is still evolving, and we would like to be involved in those discussions as it matures so we can help the client as effectively as possible. Our interest is not limited to the initial procurement of hardware and software; we want to stay engaged in the ongoing conversations about how the infrastructure support model should develop over time. By being part of that dialogue early and continuously, we can help shape a support approach that is practical, sustainable, and aligned with how the client wants to consume AI services going forward.
